How I work

The whole thing

I started SeasonCast the summer I finished high school and wrote all seven codebases myself: the iOS and Android apps, the web app, the API, and a C++ media server. Six thousand teams a year streamed through it.

Up on Friday nights

A high school football game doesn’t get a retry. SeasonCast ran three years at 99.995% uptime on servers I managed myself, under 26 minutes of downtime a year. That is still how I judge my work.

Agents on the team

On my own projects I run a small team of AI agents. Each one gets a ticket small enough to finish, a check it has to pass, and a review before anything merges. Same discipline, faster team.
