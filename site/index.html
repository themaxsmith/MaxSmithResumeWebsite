I’m a software engineer at Apple, living in Mountain View. Before that I spent three years at PlayStation in San Francisco, where I went from intern to tech lead.

I’m from Ankeny, Iowa. In 2019, the year I finished high school, I started SeasonCast, a live-streaming platform for high school and youth sports, and wrote all seven of its codebases myself, from the iOS and Android apps to a C++ media server. It grew to 300,000 monthly users and 6,000 teams a year, and held 99.995% uptime for more than three years.

On personal projects I work with AI coding agents and hold their work to the same bar as anyone’s: a small ticket, a check that has to pass, and a review before it merges.
