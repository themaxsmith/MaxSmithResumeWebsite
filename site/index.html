About

I’m a software engineer at Apple, living in Mountain View. Before that I spent three years at PlayStation in San Francisco, building features for its web, mobile and desktop apps.

I started writing software in Iowa. While studying computer science at Iowa State, I built SeasonCast, a live-streaming platform for high school and youth sports that grew to more than 300,000 viewers a month.

Most days that means TypeScript, React and React Native, with Swift, Go, Python and C++ close by. I still have a soft spot for live video.
